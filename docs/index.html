How to Install the APK on Your Phone

Because North Music is downloaded directly outside the Google Play Store, Android requires you to enable a one-time permission to install it.

"File might be harmful" or "Unknown app" warning?

This is standard on Android for any app installed from outside the Google Play Store. North Music contains zero malware, zero tracking ads, and is 100% open source. Follow the steps below to complete installation safely.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download v0.2 APK button above. When your browser (Chrome/Brave) displays "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway.

2

Enable Unknown Apps

Open the downloaded file. When prompted with "For security, your phone cannot install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings and toggle ON Allow from this source.

3

Play Protect Prompt

If Google Play Protect displays "Unrecognized app", tap More details (or the down arrow) and select Install anyway.

4

Grant Permissions

On initial launch, tap Allow for Audio files access so North Music can scan your local music library.

Manual Settings Path: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps → Tap your browser (e.g. Chrome) or Files app → Enable "Allow from this source".
Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is North Music still offline or online?

North Music is now a hybrid music player! Your local offline music library continues to work with zero internet needed, complete with equalizer, sleep timer, and folder management. When connected to the internet, you can search and stream songs directly from our 320k CDN, or download them straight into your phone storage for offline playback anytime.

Where are downloaded songs saved?

Downloaded songs are saved in standard MP3 format into your device's Music/NorthMusic directory with high-resolution album artwork and artist metadata embedded, so any audio player on your phone can recognize them.

What permissions does North Music request?

North Music only requests Read Audio Files (to scan and play your local device library), Internet / Network State (for 320k streaming, search indexing, and downloading), and Notifications (to show playback controls and sleep timer countdown in the notification drawer).

Which audio formats are supported?

North Music supports high-res FLAC, MP3, AAC, M4A, OGG Vorbis, Opus, and WAV formats with seamless gapless playback via Media3 ExoPlayer.
