How to Install the APK on Your Phone

Because North Music is downloaded directly outside the Google Play Store, Android requires you to enable a one-time permission to install it.

"File might be harmful" or "Unknown app" warning?

This is standard on Android for any app installed from outside the Google Play Store. North Music contains zero malware, zero tracking ads, and operates 100% offline. Follow the steps below to complete installation safely.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download APK button above. When your browser (Chrome/Brave) displays "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway.

2

Enable Unknown Apps

Open the downloaded file. When prompted with "For security, your phone cannot install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings and toggle ON Allow from this source.

3

Play Protect Prompt

If Google Play Protect displays "Unrecognized app", tap More details (or the down arrow) and select Install anyway.

4

Grant Permissions

On initial launch, tap Allow when requested for Audio files access so North Music can index your local music library.

Manual Settings Path: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps → Tap your browser (e.g. Chrome) or Files app → Enable "Allow from this source".
Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Why is this warning shown by Android?

Android displays the "Unknown apps" security prompt for all APK files installed directly from a web browser or file manager rather than the Google Play Store. It is a safeguard prompting user confirmation before sideloading an application package.

What permissions does North Music ask for?

North Music only requests Read Audio Files (to scan and play your device's audio files) and Notifications (Android 13+ to show playback controls and sleep timer countdown in the notification shade).

Which audio formats are supported?

North Music supports high-res FLAC, MP3, AAC, M4A, OGG Vorbis, Opus, and WAV formats with seamless gapless playback via Media3 ExoPlayer.
